Courses & Programs

Diverse academic and research opportunities in the science and practice of wellbeing

The Rekhi Centre of Excellence for the Science of Happiness offers diverse academic and research opportunities for students, educators, researchers, and professionals to engage with the science and practice of wellbeing.

Ways to Engage
📖 Enrol in academic courses and skill enhancement programmes
🎤 Participate in workshops, seminars, conferences, and training programmes
🔗 Join interdisciplinary research collaborations
🌍 Volunteer or partner with the Centre for community wellbeing initiatives
Academic Programmes Offered
Academic Programme
📗 Undergraduate (UG) and Postgraduate (PG) Courses
Type:UG / PG / Open Elective / Multidisciplinary / Skill Enhancement Courses (SEC)
Ongoing:Science of Happiness I and Science of Happiness II, offered by Department of Psychology, SoHMS, Adamas University
Research Programme
🎓 Doctoral (Ph.D.) Programme
Mode:Full-time / Part-time
Funding:Selected candidates may receive funding support from the Rekhi Foundation, USA

Suggested Transdisciplinary Research Themes

  • Health, Happiness, and Quality of Life
  • Cognitive Neuroscience and Emotional Wellbeing
  • Happiness, Urban Life, and Sustainable Development
  • Technology, AI, and Wellbeing
  • Public Policy, Governance, and Wellbeing Economics
  • Allied domain if any
Research Programme
🏅 Post-Doctoral Research Programme
Mode:Full-time
Funding:Fully/partially funded by the Rekhi Foundation, USA for suitable candidates

Suggested Transdisciplinary Research Themes

  • Advanced Measurement of Happiness and Wellbeing
  • Mental Health, Resilience, and Preventive Care
  • Happiness Interventions in Education, Healthcare, and Workplaces
  • Global and Comparative Wellbeing Studies
  • Technology, AI, and Wellbeing
  • Public Policy, Governance, and Wellbeing Economics
  • Allied domain if any
Internship Opportunity
💼 Stipend-Supported Internship Programme

The Rekhi Centre offers stipend-supported internship opportunities designed to cultivate practical skills, research experience, and professional growth in the flourishing field of wellbeing science. Supported by the Rekhi Foundation, USA, these internships provide a unique platform for motivated learners to engage deeply with interdisciplinary research, programme implementation, and community wellbeing initiatives.

Who:Undergraduate and postgraduate students from any discipline
Duration:Flexible — short-term to semester-based; part-time or full-time as per requirements
