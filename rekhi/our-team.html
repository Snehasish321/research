Our Team

The visionaries and scholars driving the science of happiness forward

Founders & Distinguished Members
Prof. (Dr.) Samit Ray
Founder Chancellor, Adamas University & Chairman, RICE Group, India
Prof. (Dr.) Samit Ray, Founder Chancellor of Adamas University and Chairman of the RICE Group, is widely regarded as one of Eastern India's most impactful education leaders. His journey started back in 1985, when he founded RICE Education with the simple dream of helping young people secure government jobs. Over the years, this initiative transformed the landscape of competitive exam coaching in West Bengal. Driven by a vision to create holistic learning environments, he established Adamas International School, Adamas World School, and in 2014, Adamas University—now a 120-acre, NAAC 'A' accredited institution known for its global outlook and interdisciplinary approach. Prof. Ray envisions the evolution of a truly research-centric institution, committed to establishing state-of-the-art facilities and distinguished Centres of Excellence. Among these, the Rekhi Centre of Excellence stands as a cherished dream—conceived to advance research, teaching, and the promotion of the science of happiness and well-being, with the noble purpose of enriching society at large.
Prof. (Dr.) Satinder Singh Rekhi
Founder, Rekhi Foundation, USA
Prof. (Dr.) Satinder Singh Rekhi is an alumnus of IIT Kharagpur and the founder of R Systems Group, a global software and services company. In 2016, Dr Rekhi and his wife Harpreet Kaur Rekhi founded the Rekhi Foundation for Happiness. The foundation's core goal is to advance the "Science of Happiness and Positive Psychology"—through establishment of dedicated centres in universities, teaching courses on happiness, conducting research via "MindLabs", organising community workshops, and embedding well-being into institutional culture. The Foundation has helped establish Happiness Centres in over 50 universities across 6 countries. Dr Rekhi views happiness not as mere emotion, but as a state of cognitive and psychological well-being that can be studied, cultivated and taught.
"Happy people are more successful than the other way around."
Prof. Manas K. Mandal
Founding Member, Rekhi Foundation, USA & Distinguished Visiting Professor, IIT KGP (Former)
Prof. Manas K. Mandal is a renowned cognitive neuropsychologist and a leading authority on the Science and Measurement of Happiness. He is currently Distinguished Visiting Professor at IIT Kharagpur and Adjunct Professor at NIAS, IISc Bengaluru, and formerly served as Distinguished Scientist and Director General (Life Sciences) at DRDO, Government of India. With extensive national and international academic experience—including Fulbright appointments at Harvard University and the University of Pennsylvania—Prof. Mandal has authored 14 books and over 100 research papers with more than 5,000 citations worldwide. For the Rekhi Centre at Adamas University, Prof. Mandal envisions a scientifically rigorous and socially relevant platform where happiness is measured, taught, and applied through evidence-based research, education, and interventions to enhance mental wellbeing, resilience, and human flourishing.
Prof. Moumita Mukherjee
Dean (R&D) and Director, Rekhi Centre of Excellence for the Science of Happiness, Adamas University
Dr. Moumita Mukherjee is a distinguished academician and scientist, serving as the Director and Chairperson of the Rekhi Centre of Excellence for the Science of Happiness at Adamas University. An alumna of Presidency College and the University of Calcutta, she holds an M.Sc. in Physics, M.Tech. in Biomedical Engineering, and a Ph.D. (Tech.) in Radio Physics and Electronics. She has served as a Senior Scientist at DRDO, Ministry of Defence, and held prestigious international research positions at Newcastle University, UK, and the Technical University of Darmstadt, Germany. With more than 160 peer-reviewed publications, her research spans THz electronics, biomedical engineering, nano-biosensors, and medical instrumentation. Under her leadership, the Rekhi Centre integrates scientific inquiry with emotional intelligence, mindfulness, and wellbeing, fostering a campus culture where happiness is actively cultivated alongside academic excellence.
