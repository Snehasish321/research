Our Centre & Lab

Rekhi Centre of Excellence for the Science of Happiness Lab

The Rekhi Centre is housed on the 3rd Floor of the Central Research Development Center (CRDC), Adamas University. The Centre features:

🏛️A dedicated conference hall for seminars, workshops, and scholarly dialogues
💻Smart classrooms equipped with interactive teaching and learning technologies
📡Wi-Fi-enabled workspaces supporting collaborative research and training programmes

Mind Lab Facility

The Centre also hosts a Mind Lab, a specialized space designed to support research, training, and experiential learning in mental wellbeing, mindfulness, emotional regulation, and cognitive resilience. The Mind Lab facilitates evidence-based practices and reflective learning, enabling participants to engage with scientifically grounded tools for enhancing wellbeing and happiness.

State-of-the-Art Research Infrastructure

Adamas University offers advanced research infrastructure to support large-scale academic events and high-quality research activities. The University houses well-equipped research laboratories with cutting-edge instruments and technologies, enabling interdisciplinary research across science, engineering, health, and social sciences.

Auditoriums and Conference Facilities

The University has multiple auditoriums and conference halls with a combined seating capacity of over 2,000 participants. These venues are equipped with modern audio-visual systems, digital presentation facilities, and seamless connectivity.

Accommodation Facilities

The University provides on-campus accommodation facilities capable of hosting up to 1,000 participants, ensuring safe, comfortable, and convenient lodging for visiting scholars, researchers, and conference delegates.
